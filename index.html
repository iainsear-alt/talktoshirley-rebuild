Talk to Shirley Counselling Psychotherapist in Winchester Hampshire

Hello! I'm Shirley. I am a psychotherapist and counsellor, with over 20 years experience, specialising in anxiety, trauma and relationships, working with adults and young people (13+) in central Winchester.

Applying cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) based psychotherapeutic methodology, which is a powerful mix of life coaching, counselling and psychotherapy, I can move you towards optimal mental wellbeing with an unshakable self-belief that will ignite your strengths, resources and creativity to get you the life you want.

Using a blend of humour, fresh perspectives, insightfulness and creativity all wrapped up in 100% commitment, I can help you get to where you would like to be. Come talk to me.

About me

I love meeting people and have a real passion for helping people reach their optimum mental wellbeing. I am a qualified counsellor, psychotherapist, psychotherapeutic coach and workshop facilitator with experience in supporting young people from age 13 years plus and adult mental health and wellbeing.

As a registered member of the British Association for Counselling & Psychotherapy (BACP) I work within the BACP Ethical framework.

I have been told I am an upbeat, insightful, approachable person. I aim to offer a service that's trustworthy, confidential and beneficial for you.

I have qualifications across the spectrum of counselling, psychotherapy and trauma-resolution interventions. How To workshops

My range of How To Workshops have been created to suit either one person or a small group. They have proven very popular as they are delivered in a friendly, approachable, confidential space.

The workshops focus on building an understanding of key concepts and putting into practice simple useful skills and techniques. You can take away these tools right from the first session.

  • How to improve your self-confidence
  • How to improve your communication skills
  • How to lift your mood quickly
  • How to cope with anxiety (including social anxiety and panic attacks)
  • How to use the knowledge and understanding of how your brain works to help with stress, anger, depression and anxiety
  • How to use relaxation and mindfulness to cope with the stresses and strains of living
  • How to use the power of laughter therapy to feel really good
